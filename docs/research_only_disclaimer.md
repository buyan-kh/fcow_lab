# Research-Only Disclaimer

Frontier Bio Genome to Mechanism is a synthetic-data research and education tool. It does not diagnose disease, establish personal disease risk, predict a medical future, recommend treatment, recommend a drug or dosage, or design a drug for an individual.

Do not use this output to change medication or make health decisions. Consult a qualified clinician or genetic counselor for health questions. Production genomic uploads require security, consent, legal, and clinical review. Evidence may be conflicting, incomplete, model-limited, or missing; hypotheses require experiments.
