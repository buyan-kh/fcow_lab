# Frontier Bio Genome to Mechanism

Frontier Bio is a research and education workspace for exploring how synthetic genetic variation may relate to biological mechanisms and disease-level therapeutic hypotheses. It is not a consumer DNA interpretation app, diagnostic system, clinical decision tool, or personalized treatment recommender.

The first wedge is a transparent decision loop:

```text
synthetic genome -> normalized variants -> public-evidence fixtures
  -> gene/pathway context -> mechanism hypothesis
  -> therapeutic research hypothesis -> validation experiment -> report
```

The long-term company vision is an AI-native pharmaceutical operating system for serious biotech teams. The first product must earn that ambition by showing uncertainty, provenance, and the next experiment rather than by generating unsupported drug claims.
