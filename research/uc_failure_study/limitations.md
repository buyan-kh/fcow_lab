# Limitations

- GSE14580 contains only 24 UC patients, with 8 responders and 16 nonresponders.
- The study is single-center/single-accession and uses one treatment: infliximab.
- The response label is source-defined healing at 4–6 weeks; primary nonresponse and later loss of response are not separable from the available metadata.
- The series matrix is probe-level; this first analysis does not claim gene-level annotation or causal biology.
- A 25% holdout from 24 patients produces a very small test set. Metrics have high uncertainty and are not clinical performance estimates.
- There is no independent validation cohort in GSE14580. Any apparent pattern requires replication in another public cohort.
- Treatment exposure, dose variation, concurrent therapies, disease severity details, and other confounders are incompletely represented in the public metadata.
- Public access to GEO does not imply permission to re-identify subjects or link them to external records. No re-identification is attempted.
- Patentability, freedom to operate, safety, efficacy, and clinical utility are outside this study.
