# Personal Wellness Sleep Boundary

## Scope of this phase

Personal Wellness is a local proof loop for one sleep behavior experiment. It accepts synthetic fixtures and manually entered demo values in browser memory only. It does not process real medical records, pathology reports, molecular reports, personal DNA, prescriptions, medication records, device credentials, or wearable connections.

The prototype does not diagnose sleep disorders, make personal medical risk claims, prescribe, recommend dosage, or recommend prescription drugs. The intervention is a reversible behavior protocol: moving caffeine earlier for a fixed observation period.

## Recommendation contract

Every learning report presents these sections in order:

1. Here is the goal.
2. Here is the baseline.
3. Here is the intervention.
4. Here is why it may be relevant.
5. Here is what we measured.
6. Here is what changed.
7. Here are the limitations.
8. Here is what to discuss with a clinician if symptoms persist.

The only next-step dispositions are **keep**, **modify**, or **stop** the behavior experiment. A disposition is a bounded product decision based on observed fixture or local demo values; it is not a medical conclusion.

## Sequencing

- Personal Wellness is the only functional mode in this phase.
- Explore is a placeholder for future source-backed evidence retrieval.
- Clinical Navigation is a placeholder for future synthetic cancer, ulcer, and gallstone cases.
- Population-level responder analysis and therapeutic discovery are later governed layers that require explicit consent, de-identification, security, legal review, and scientific oversight.

## Visual and interaction constraint

The product uses neutral rules, spacing, and typography for hierarchy. It does not use blue left rails, tinted callout cards, decorative accent borders, gradients, or a generic AI health-coach pattern.
